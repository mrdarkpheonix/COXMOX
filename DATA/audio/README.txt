THE COXMOX - ORIGINAL INSTRUMENT LOOPS
======================================

These local WAV files are short, original synthesized loops made for this
project. They are not downloaded recordings or samples from commercial
music. Each event category has its own melody and timbre. The app plays
these local files directly, without a remote audio host.

wildfires.wav                 Marimba
severe-storms.wav             French horn
volcanoes.wav                 Pipe organ
floods.wav                    Piano
sea-lake-ice.wav              Glockenspiel
earthquakes.wav               Timpani
drought.wav                   Flute
dust-haze.wav                 Clarinet
landslides.wav                Cello
snow.wav                      Celesta
temperature-extremes.wav      Muted trumpet
water-color.wav               Harp
manmade.wav                   Synth bass
chimes.wav                    Fallback for other categories

The loops are played locally from this folder. Event intensity changes the
playback rate slightly; it is not represented by louder audio.
